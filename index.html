90s R&B

Slow-jam R&B kits: swung sixteenth hats, doubled backbeats, ninth chords and a vocal-range motif at 76 to 96 BPM.

Status: No human listening audition was performed for this build. The audio and the film are preview, not a master. They are a simple synthesized render of the MIDI, not a recording and not a mix. No license is set.

How to use the files

Build from the drums and bass, then lay the motif where a vocal line would sit.

Use either the combined arrangement or the separate parts. Do not stack the part files on top of the combined file.

No program changes are encoded. A conventional GM player will normally use its default piano program for pitched channels unless configured otherwise; compatible GM channel 10 percussion is treated separately. Player behavior may vary.
